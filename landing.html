How it works

From a hiring post to an interview

You set the rules once. Outreach finds, applies, follows up and keeps score.

Find openings

Hiring posts from LinkedIn, Hacker News and the web, jobs from company boards and job platforms, and the people at any company you type.

Reach out and apply

Personal emails from your own Gmail, sent a few each day, plus automatic applications on Naukri, Instahyre and Hirist to jobs that score above your bar.

Never miss a reply

Replies and bounces are read back from your inbox, follow-ups go out when nobody answers, and the people waiting on you go to the top.

Track to the offer

Interviews with stages, dates and reminders, and analytics on which sources and templates actually get answers.

Features

Everything a job search needs, in one place

No more spreadsheets, copied emails and forgotten follow-ups.

Leads

Hiring posts from LinkedIn, Hacker News, web search or text you paste in, each with the recruiter's email.

Auto-apply

Up to three times a day it pulls new jobs from Naukri, Instahyre and Hirist, scores each one against your profile, and applies to the ones above your bar. It answers screening questions from your saved answers, keeps your profile fresh, and tells you who viewed or shortlisted you. Open roles from company boards like Greenhouse and Lever show up too.

Discover

Type a company, see who works there, and work out their email address from the company's email format.

Campaigns

Choose how many emails go out each day and at what time. If Gmail hits its daily limit, sending pauses and picks up again later.

Smart mailbox

AI reads every reply and sorts it: asking for your resume, reviewing, needs attention, stay in touch, or a no. Out-of-office auto-replies are filtered out first. The ones waiting on you land in “Needs you”, with an AI-drafted reply you edit and send in place.

Interviews

Follow each conversation from first reply to offer, with stages, dates and reminders.

Analytics

See reply rates by template, company and source, plus a weekly PDF report sent to your inbox.

Blocklist

Addresses and whole companies that must never be emailed, enforced on every send.

One-click actions

Ready-made workflows, like a step-by-step send or a whole campaign, start from a button on any page.

Your data

Your inbox, your keys, your call

Outreach sends as you, not through a shared server address, so people reply to you and your sender reputation stays your own.

  • Your own Gmail. Connected with an App Password that is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and used only to send and read your mail.
  • Your own AI keys. Drafts and scoring use your own free Groq or Gemini key.
  • Your rules, enforced. Auto-apply only goes after jobs above the score you choose, within your daily limits, and a guessed email address is never sent until you approve it.
  • Sign in without a password. A six-digit code is emailed to you each time you sign in.
